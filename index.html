About

I am an embedded software engineer from Serbia with more than four years of experience at Continental and Aumovio.

I started as a system test engineer, moved into embedded software development, and now work as a software architect leading a five-member team. Along the way I have worked on several automotive ECUs, across the full development cycle.

I hold an MSc in Automotive Software from the Faculty of Technical Sciences in Novi Sad. My thesis on machine learning was published at IEEE ZINC 2025. I am most interested in low-level programming, embedded Linux, operating systems, wireless communication and AI on edge devices.
